# Task Group 5: Disentangled Attention Fusion

Explain how the four multimodal representations are built.

## Examples of questions to be covered

- Explain how the four representations Z_gg, Z_hh, Z_hg and Z_gh are formed, and what information they aim to capture.
- Explain the mechanics of attention here: what are the queries, keys and values in the cross-attention layer that produces Z_gh?
- Explain the aggregation layer. How are the attention weights computed and used, and what is the final representation that goes into the risk predictor?
- The paper checks that all four representations are actually used. What would it mean if they were not, and why would that undermine the disentanglement results?
- What are alternative fusion strategies? What would be gained or lost?
